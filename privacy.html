Privacy policy: GertrudeWorks pipeline

Last updated 16 October 2026.

The GertrudeWorks pipeline is a private tool used only by the owner of the GertrudeWorks shop to build spreadsheet templates. It is not offered to anyone else.

Google account data

  • The tool signs in to the owner's own Google account only, with two permissions: drive.file (only the Drive files the tool itself creates) and spreadsheets (to write and check the template spreadsheets it creates).
  • It creates template spreadsheets in the owner's Drive, reads them back to test their formulas, and shares the finished templates by link so buyers can make their own copy.
  • It does not read any other file, email, contact or calendar data, and it does not collect data about other people.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. No Google data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or to train models.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
